## English

### What Is This?

It's 11pm before your launch. The PH page is ready, but you don't actually know what moves the ranking — upvotes? comments? time of day? This playbook is the operations manual for the next 24 hours and the 72 after that, reverse-engineered from coaching 30+ #1 finishes.

### What's Inside

- **Ranking algorithm deep-dive** — it's not just upvotes; weighted factors for the first 3 hours, first 24 hours, and weekly ranking
- **Launch-day battle plan** — minute-by-minute checklist with exact PST timing windows (the first 3 hours are decisive)
- **Pre-launch asset prep** — gallery optimization, product URL selection, tagline refinement (≤60 chars), maker comment templates
- **Community mobilization** — ethical, anti-spam-safe ways to activate your network; what PH detects and penalizes
- **Post-launch 72h momentum plan** — converting PH traffic into retained users; conditions for Weekly #1
- **Case studies** — three categories dissected (AI tool, app, SaaS)

### 这是什么？

发布前夜 11 点，你的 PH 页面已就绪，但你并不知道排名到底由什么驱动——点赞？评论？发布时间？这份手册是接下来 24 小时（以及之后 72 小时）的作战手册，从 30+ 次日榜第一的辅导实战中逆向还原。

### 内容清单

- **排名算法拆解**——不只是 upvote：前 3 小时、24 小时、周榜的加权因子
- **发布日作战计划**——精确到 PST 时间窗口的逐小时清单（前 3 小时定胜负）
- **发布前资产准备**——gallery 优化、产品 URL 选择、tagline 打磨（≤60 字符）、maker comment 模板
- **社区动员**——合规、防 spam 判定的网络激活方式；PH 检测与惩罚机制
- **发布后 72 小时冲量计划**——把 PH 流量转化为留存用户；冲周榜第一的条件
- **案例拆解**——AI 工具 / App / SaaS 三个品类
